# Welcome to BioMedGPS

BioMedGPS is a comprehensive knowledge graph platform that leverages graph neural networks for drug repurposing, disease mechanism discovery, and biomedical knowledge exploration. Our platform integrates multiple data sources and provides powerful tools for researchers, clinicians, and data scientists working in the biomedical domain.

## Key Features

- **Knowledge Graph Exploration**: Interactive graph visualization and querying capabilities
- **Drug Repurposing**: AI-powered predictions for drug-target interactions and therapeutic applications
- **Disease Mechanism Analysis**: Deep insights into disease pathways and molecular mechanisms
- **Omics Data Integration**: Support for multi-omics data analysis and interpretation
- **Personalized Knowledge Graphs**: Create and curate custom knowledge bases
- **AI-Powered Chat**: Ask questions and get answers from scientific literature

## What Makes BioMedGPS Special?

### Comprehensive Knowledge Integration
BioMedGPS integrates data from multiple biomedical databases and literature sources, providing a unified view of biological knowledge.

### AI-Powered Insights
Our graph neural networks and machine learning models help identify hidden patterns and generate novel hypotheses for drug discovery and disease research.

### Interactive Visualization
Explore complex biological networks through intuitive, interactive graph visualizations that make it easy to understand relationships between genes, diseases, drugs, and other biomedical entities.

### Scalable and Customizable
Whether you're working with standard databases or your own proprietary data, BioMedGPS can scale to meet your needs.
